Our Process

From Discover
to Distribute.

A seven-step workflow that puts craft, culture and audience at the heart of every release.

Step 01

Discover

Story hunts, IP scouting and cultural research.

Step 02

Develop

Writers’ room, story bible and franchise blueprint.

Step 03

Design

Concept art, worldbuilding and pre-visualisation.

Step 04

Produce

Principal photography with big-screen craft.

Step 05

Polish

Editorial, sound, VFX and colour on event-scale specs.

Step 06

Position

Marketing, PR and audience-first campaigns.

Step 07

Distribute

Pan-Indian and global theatrical release.
